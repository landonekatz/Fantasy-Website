Personnel Changes

The whole league was distraught to learn of perennial loser Jack Lovas's removal. It was not due to their love of Lovas, as the loss of a free win one could pencil in their schedule.


Lovas's replacement, Madoc Watson, passed the Commissioner and Committee's physical and mental examinations and will debut in the opening week of the 2026 season. Watson is known for his delusional self-belief and scrappy playstyle and projects as a bottom-to-middle level contender.

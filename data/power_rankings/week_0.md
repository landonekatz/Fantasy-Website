<div>
    <h3>1. Will</h3>

    <p>
        <strong>Positives:</strong><br>
        Good QB with big upside. Check. Stud WR1s. Check. Strong RBs. Check. Kyle Pitts. Hell yeah.
        Will’s team checks all the boxes for a powerhouse – and even if players underperform, the floor is high.
    </p>

    <p>
        <strong>Negatives:</strong><br>
        Double XP weekend and meditation man, Pickens eating into CeeDee’s touches, player on the Jets.
    </p>

    <h3>2. Jordan</h3>

    <p>
        <strong>Positives:</strong><br>
        The one-man offense known formally as Jahmir Gibbs. Trey McBreezy - he makes that shit look easy.
        Dak/Pickens stack. Knuckles free from Sonic’s shackles. And they say that it’s Parker Washington’s year.
    </p>

    <p>
        <strong>Negatives:</strong><br>
        DMont, McBride, and Judkins are all on bad offensive teams. The upside of these guys could get capped
        pretty quickly if their QBs can’t move the ball. Also, the only true WR1 on Jordan’s team is Alec Pierce.
        Not that Pickens doesn’t get the touches of a 1, but still something to keep an eye on.
    </p>

    <h3>3. Madoc</h3>

    <p>
        <strong>Positives:</strong><br>
        Week One most projected points! Hang the banner in the rafters! Let me chill. Madoc’s got some guys.
        JT/Henry is a great 1-2 punch at RB, Warren is excellent (and might get some goal line work with sneaks,
        considering that they want to keep Jones healthy), Hurts will get his tush pushed, and the WR room is
        young, hungry, and keeping family members quiet on their quarterbacks (for now).
    </p>

    <p>
        <strong>Negatives:</strong><br>
        Eagles offense seems primed for regression. Hurts is always a high floor guy, but he really might not
        have much of a ceiling. Bryce Young needs to air that MF out more to fully unlock Tet – will he?
        Age has to get to the King eventually. People are saying that he looked old in training camp.
    </p>

    <h3>4. Luke</h3>

    <p>
        <strong>Positives:</strong><br>
        That’s a damn good WR room. Physical, incredibly talented guys who demand targets and are huge
        big-play threats. Lamar Jackson. Javonte coming off a big season – and decent contract extension –
        with a healthy Dallas O-line. Likely freed from the shackles of Mandrews. Lot of things to like here.
    </p>

    <p>
        <strong>Negatives:</strong><br>
        It’s Luke’s team. Something will happen. I really don’t love Swift this year, and Jamo struggles
        with volume due to the sun god.
    </p>

    <h3>5. Jake</h3>

    <p>
        <strong>Positives:</strong><br>
        Kind of a lot of possible outcomes with this team. Jamaar and Nico are obviously elite – and Jamaar
        can keep you in it by himself – but there are some issues there with QB health (and CJ Stroud).
        Rice is great when on the field. Drake Maye is great when the schedule cooperates. Bucky Irving is
        great when not depressed. For now, this is a strong unit. Also a Jake-drafted team, so I’ll give him
        the benefit of the doubt.
    </p>

    <p>
        <strong>Negatives:</strong><br>
        See above.
    </p>

    <h3>6. Mike</h3>

    <p>
        <strong>Positives:</strong><br>
        Great bellcow backs – one of, if not the best RB rooms in the league. Bijan and Jacobs will be
        dominating touches all year – and there’s no one to steal Bijan’s touchdowns this time.
        Married man Kelce. Married man Josh Allen. Married man Cam Skattebo (he’s currently in a loving
        relationship with head trauma – they have five kids, and all of them bite).
    </p>

    <p>
        <strong>Negatives:</strong><br>
        A Mike-managed team normally has bad receivers. This is the worst it’s ever been.
        DJ “Amari Cooper” Moore in the fourth would be the reach of the draft if Carson didn’t draft a literal
        corner. Touchdowns will most likely be necessary for that group to not be total ass.
    </p>

    <h3>7. Carson</h3>

    <p>
        <strong>Positives:</strong><br>
        Three letters. J. S. N. He was an absolute monster last year, and without K9, Seattle could lean on
        him even more. Bowers should be in a better situation this year – doesn’t hurt that they have no
        receivers – and Love could be really fun to watch this year. Rest of the roster is filled out with
        typically consistent, established options.
    </p>

    <p>
        <strong>Negatives:</strong><br>
        I really don’t love the ceiling. It just feels like this team isn’t going to really be capable of
        putting up big points on a weekly basis. Lot of guys on here with question marks surrounding them,
        their teams, their general situations – we’ll see.
    </p>

    <h3>8. Ben</h3>

    <p>
        <strong>Positives:</strong><br>
        This feels like a weird team for Ben to have. But the upside can’t be denied.
        DeVonta and Burden (essentially) WR1 seasons, Chase Brown true breakout looks lined up,
        James Cook coming off a career year, and Mike Evans with an opportunity to get a shit ton of targets.
    </p>

    <p>
        <strong>Negatives:</strong><br>
        This is a team that could move up in these rankings quickly – they could just as easily stay here
        or go down. Burden had an injury in training camp that was serious enough to make playing in week one
        a concern, no one knows if Hurts and the Eagles offense will be capable of getting the ball to Smith
        enough to make him fantasy relevant in their current state, and Mike Evans is Mike Evans + substation.
        Chase Brown also hasn’t had a full season of being a good fantasy football player yet.
    </p>

    <h3>9. Landon</h3>

    <p>
        <strong>Positives:</strong><br>
        Taking the players other people don’t want to touch leads to a damn talented roster.
        CMC, AJ Brown, Nabers, Garrett Willson – Landon’s team won’t be lacking in skill.
    </p>

    <p>
        <strong>Negatives:</strong><br>
        There are reasons others don’t want them. Brown has been a fantasy liability for the last few seasons,
        CMC’s workload appears to be ridiculous – and he’s 30. He and Nabers both have massive health concerns
        associated with them. And I think if you asked Garrett Wilson right now, he would tell you not to draft him.
        Somebody free that guy!
    </p>

    <h3>10. Isabella</h3>

    <p>
        <strong>Positives:</strong><br>
        Ra. Helios. Apollo. Sol. Amon-Ra St. Brown. All masters at their craft.
        Outside of the Sun God, it feels like Isabella’s team has a lot of players poised for breakout seasons –
        K9, Zay, Loveland, Henderson.
    </p>

    <p>
        <strong>Negatives:</strong><br>
        It’s called a breakout season because the player hasn’t really broken out yet.
        K9 has struggled to stay healthy throughout his career – and he’s got a big workload lined up in KC.
        Henderson has to deal with Rhamondre for some reason. Lots of mouths to feed in Chicago.
        And there’s a reason people say “Why can’t we get Lamar a real WR1?” every year.
    </p>

    <h3>11. Ryan</h3>

    <p>
        <strong>Positives:</strong><br>
        Ryan’s got the YAC team this year. When guys like Egbuka, McConkey, Saquon, and Achane get their
        hands on the ball, fun things happen. And everyone apparently now thinks Joe Burrow is a top-three
        quarterback in the NFL – and his team has no defense. Lots and lots of points there.
    </p>

    <p>
        <strong>Negatives:</strong><br>
        These guys don’t really score touchdowns. That’s the main reason this team is down here.
        Ever since the tush push, Saquon’s basically had to house it. Baker spreads the wealth too much
        when they get down there – but no Mike Evans might help a bit. McConkey is an open-field guy.
        Achane will be on a terrible team with a big rushing QB who doesn’t throw as many checkdowns as Tua.
        Dowdle is on the Steelers – they just suck.
    </p>

    <h3>12. Alex</h3>

    <p>
        <strong>Positives:</strong><br>
        Young, fun running backs with explosive potential. Big WRs who are serious red zone threats in strong
        offenses. Bo Nix’s WR1!
    </p>

    <p>
        <strong>Negatives:</strong><br>
        If someone told me I drafted this team I wouldn’t be shocked. Because it looks like it was drafted by
        a gambling man. Higgins had very low volume last year (less than four receptions a game!) and got saved
        by touchdowns. Jeanty literally got smoked in the backfield every other play. Hampton was good when he
        played – left ankle fracture and right ankle sprain. Watson is another low volume guy – 3.5 rec per game!
        And Waddle’s already missing practices. Taking a lot of chances here.
    </p>

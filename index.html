Platform Offerings

Why We Outclass Generic Platforms
01 • Box Score Archives

Detailed Matchup Cards

Other services: Show you only final scores and basic totals.

We show you everything — full starting lineups, bench rosters, individual player stat lines, weekly projections vs actual scoring, and real NFL game context for every single matchup in your league's history.

02 • Automated Analytics

Living Record Books

Other platforms: Force you to manually calculate records or lose them when a new season starts.

Our living record books automatically calculate single-game high and low scores, longest winning and losing streaks, playoff milestones, and championship histories that refresh live as new week scores come in.

03 • Group Chat Banter

Weekly League Newsletters

Other apps: Leave your league completely dormant from Tuesday to Sunday.

League members can opt in to weekly newsletters and narrative digests that automatically surface emerging matchup storylines, manager trends, and talking points to keep group chat banter alive.

04 • League Culture

Engaging League Traditions

Other sites: Treat fantasy football like a sterile weekly spreadsheet.

Introduce fresh traditions to your league — including member-curated Power Rankings, dedicated Rivalry Weeks, all-time head-to-head matchup matrices, and custom trophies. These are features we built and tested in our own league, now shared with yours.

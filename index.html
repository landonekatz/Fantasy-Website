How The Fantasy Vault Works

From Day 1 to Championship Sunday
STEP 01

Connect Your League

Enter your ESPN League ID or connect Yahoo Fantasy. The Fantasy Vault imports your complete history, as past seasons, week-by-week scores, rosters, draft picks, and managers.

STEP 02

Instant Historical Archive

Your league instantly gets a dedicated URL (thefantasyvault.com/yourleague) featuring all-time head-to-head records, single-game scoring records, and championship standings.

STEP 03

Automated Continuous Updates

As new games finish each week, your record books, matchup cards, and manager stats update automatically, keeping your league archive alive season after season.

Platform Offerings & Features

Everything Included in Your Archive
01 • Complete Matchup History

Detailed Matchup Cards

We store every game ever played in your league. Revisit full starting lineups, bench rosters, individual player stat lines, weekly projections vs. actual scoring, and NFL game context.

02 • All-Time Record Tracking

Living Record Books

Track single-game high and low scores, longest winning and losing streaks, playoff milestones, and championship histories that update automatically as new scores come in.

03 • Member Communication

Weekly League Newsletters

League members can opt in to weekly email newsletters highlighting key matchup narratives, manager trends, and talking points to keep banter active in your group chat.

04 • Custom Culture & Banter

League Traditions & Rivalries

Introduce fresh traditions to your league, as including member-curated Power Rankings, dedicated Rivalry Weeks, all-time head-to-head matchup matrices, and custom trophies built from our own league experience.

Manual Developer Tools Guide
  1. Log into your ESPN league on desktop at fantasy.espn.com.
  2. Right-click anywhere and select Inspect (or press F12).
  3. Go to the Application tab (Chrome/Edge) or Storage tab (Firefox).
  4. Expand Cookies → select https://fantasy.espn.com.
  5. Copy the Value for espn_s2 and SWID into the fields above.

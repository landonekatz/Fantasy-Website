How The Fantasy Vault Works

From Day 1 to Championship Sunday
STEP 01

Connect Your League

Enter your ESPN League ID or connect Yahoo Fantasy. The Fantasy Vault imports your complete history — past seasons, week-by-week scores, rosters, draft picks, and managers.

STEP 02

Instant Historical Archive

Your league instantly gets a dedicated URL (e.g. thefantasyvault.com/yourleague) featuring all-time head-to-head records, single-game scoring records, and championship standings.

STEP 03

Automated Continuous Updates

As new games finish each week, your record books, matchup cards, and manager stats update automatically, keeping your league archive alive season after season.

Private ESPN Authentication

To connect to a private ESPN league, we temporarily require your s2 and SWID browser cookies. We are fully transparent: these values are used strictly to authenticate the one-time data sync with ESPN. We do not store them.

How to find these cookies
  1. Log into your ESPN league on a desktop browser.
  2. Right-click anywhere on the page and select Inspect.
  3. Go to the Application or Storage tab.
  4. Expand the Cookies menu and click on https://fantasy.espn.com.
  5. Find the rows named swid and espn_s2. Copy their Values into these fields.

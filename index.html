Platform Offerings

Built for League Engagement
01

Detailed Matchup Cards

Complete box scores featuring starter rosters, individual player stat lines, projections vs actual scoring, and NFL week game results for every matchup in your league's history.

02

Active Weekly Record Books

Single-game high and low scoring records, longest winning and losing streaks, playoff milestones, and championship history that automatically refresh as new scores come in.

03

Weekly League Newsletters

Members can opt in to receive weekly league digests highlighting emerging matchup narratives, top manager performances, and talking points to keep banter alive in the group chat.

04

Fresh Traditions & Engagement

Introduce proven traditions to your league — including member-curated Power Rankings, dedicated Rivalry Weeks, all-time head-to-head records, and custom trophies. These are features we created for our own league and are excited to share with yours.
